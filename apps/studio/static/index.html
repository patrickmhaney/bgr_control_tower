How It Works

How a reporting need goes from a business request to working numbers, and how it stays current after that.

Business Data team Together
  1. 1

    Define

    Describe the business processes, the measures that matter, how to break them down, and sketch each dashboard.

  2. 2

    Review

    Give every measure an owner, answer open questions and agree which measures are ready to build.

  3. 3

    Build

    Connect the source systems, model the data and implement each measure exactly as defined.

  4. 4

    Test

    Check every measure against an expected answer. A number that changes is flagged and explained.

  5. 5

    Run

    Load the source data each business day and refresh every number and dashboard.

  6. 6

    Use and sign off

    Review the dashboards, compare them with trusted reports and approve each measure.

Keep it current. New requests and changes start again at step 1. Wording and ownership changes take effect straight away; a change to how a number is calculated is reviewed by the business and the data team, then rebuilt and retested.

The life of a measure

The status each measure carries, shown on the Requirements and Governance pages.

  1. placeholderDefined by the business, not built yet.
  2. provisionalBuilt and tested, not yet checked against a trusted report.
  3. activeSigned off by its owner. The official number.

blocked The data isn't in any connected system yet. The measure waits until it is.
